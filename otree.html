oTree code repository

A collection of oTree* tasks for experimental economics, created, improved, or customized by me.

* Chen, D. L., Schonger, M., & Wickens, C. (2016). oTree — An open-source platform for laboratory, online, and field experiments. Journal of Behavioral and Experimental Finance, 9, 88–97.

Norm Following Task
Participants allocate balls between two buckets, balancing an explicit social norm against a higher personal payoff. Based on Kimbrough & Vostroknutov (2018), for oTree Lite.
Learn more
Cite this tool

Optimism Task
A card-based task to study optimistic beliefs: participants choose a card and predict how many cards in a sample share its color. Based on Corazzini, Filippin & Vanin (2015), for oTree Lite.
Learn more
Cite this tool

Atzori, F. (n.d.). Optimism Task [Computer software]. GitHub. https://github.com/federicoatz/oTree-Optimism-Task

StairCase Lottery
Elicits risk preferences through repeated choices between a lottery and a sure payment that adjusts after each decision. Based on Holzmeister & Pfurtscheller (2016), for oTree Lite.
Learn more
Cite this tool

Atzori, F. (n.d.). Staircase Risk Elicitation [Computer software]. GitHub. https://github.com/federicoatz/oTree-Staircase-Risk-Elicitation

Public Good Game
Interactive practice rounds for a public goods game, with and without costly punishment. Participants explore contributions and resulting payoffs with simulated co-players before the experiment.
